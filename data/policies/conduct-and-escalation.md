# Workplace Conduct and Escalation Policy

## Respectful workplace
Employees are expected to communicate respectfully and avoid harassment, discrimination, retaliation, and intimidation. Concerns may be raised with a manager or People Operations.

## Raising a concern
An employee may contact People Operations directly if the concern involves their manager or if they are uncomfortable raising it with that manager. Share only information relevant to the concern and use approved private channels. This sample does not describe investigation procedures or determine outcomes.

## Urgent safety concern
For an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact local emergency services. Notify the company security help channel when safe to do so. The assistant is not an emergency response service.
