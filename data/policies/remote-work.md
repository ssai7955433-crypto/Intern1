# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose and scope
This fictional policy describes flexible-work arrangements for the demo company, Northstar Demo Co. It applies to regular employees in roles approved for remote work. Local employment law and signed agreements take precedence over this sample.

## Requesting a remote-work arrangement
An employee must submit a planned remote-work arrangement to their manager at least five business days before it starts. The manager reviews role coverage, data-handling needs, and team coordination, then records the decision in the team's approved HR system. A request is not approved until the manager confirms it in writing.

## Equipment and work location
Company-owned devices must be used to access company systems. Employees must work from an approved location and protect screens and conversations from people who are not authorized to see company information. Reimbursement for home-office costs is not specified by this sample policy; consult the current People Operations guidance.

## Availability
Remote employees follow their team's agreed working hours and remain reachable through approved communication channels during those hours. Adjustments for accessibility, caregiving, or local requirements should be discussed privately with People Operations.
