# Business Expense Policy

## Eligible expenses
Reasonable business expenses that are necessary for an approved business purpose may be reimbursed when the employee follows the approval and documentation steps in this policy. Personal purchases, commuting costs, and expenses without a business purpose are not reimbursable under this sample.

## Approval and submission
Obtain manager approval before making any single purchase over 250 USD. Submit an expense report in the approved finance system within 30 calendar days after the expense. Include an itemized receipt and a short business-purpose description. Finance may return incomplete submissions for correction.

## Travel
Travel must be approved by the manager before booking. Use economy-class transport and reasonably priced lodging unless a documented accessibility or operational need requires an exception. This sample does not define per-diem rates, currency conversion rules, or emergency travel procedures; ask Finance before booking if those details matter.

## Reimbursement timing
Finance reviews complete submissions. This sample does not promise a fixed payment date. Employees can ask Finance for a status update through the approved finance help channel.
