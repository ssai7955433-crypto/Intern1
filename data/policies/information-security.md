# Information Security Policy

## Account and device security
Use a unique password and multi-factor authentication for company accounts. Lock the screen when stepping away. Install security updates promptly and do not disable endpoint protection.

## Handling sensitive information
Store company information only in approved company-managed systems. Do not upload confidential, personal, or regulated information to public AI tools, personal cloud storage, or unapproved applications. Share documents only with people who need access for an authorized business purpose.

## Suspected incident
Report a lost device, suspected account compromise, misdirected sensitive message, or other suspected security incident immediately through the company security help channel. Do not investigate by accessing another person's account or deleting evidence. If there is immediate danger to a person, contact local emergency services first.

## Access requests
Request access through the approved IT service process and include the business purpose. Managers approve role-based access. Never share credentials or use another person's account.
