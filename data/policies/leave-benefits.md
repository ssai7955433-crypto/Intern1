# Leave and Benefits Policy

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per calendar year, prorated in their first year. Requests should be submitted in the HR system at least ten business days before planned leave. A manager confirms coverage and approval in writing. This sample does not define carryover, payout, or statutory leave entitlements.

## Sick leave
Employees who cannot work due to illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably practical and record the absence in the HR system. Medical details should not be sent through team chat. A manager may direct questions about documentation requirements to People Operations; this sample does not set a medical-documentation threshold.

## Parental leave
The demo company provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave to eligible employees following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Employees should contact People Operations at least 30 days before the expected leave where practicable. Eligibility and coordination with statutory benefits must be confirmed with People Operations because this sample is not a complete benefits plan.

## Benefits questions
Coverage, enrollment windows, dependent eligibility, and plan-specific terms are not defined in this sample. People Operations is the designated contact for benefits questions.
